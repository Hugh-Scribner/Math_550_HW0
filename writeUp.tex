\section{Problem 3}
 \begin{tcolorbox}
        \textbf{Problem Statement:}\\
        Consider the ODE
        \begin{equation}
            \frac{d^2u}{dx^2} + sin(x)\frac{du}{dx} + u(x) = f(x)
            \label{eqn: prob 3}
        \end{equation}
        defined on $0 \leq x \leq 5$. Solve the ODE using second-order finite difference methods with the Dirichlet conditions. Use $u(x) = sin(x)$ to test your solution by finding boundary conditions and the necessary forcing function $f(x)$. Show an error plot that proves your method converges with second order.
        
    \end{tcolorbox}
    
    \textbf{Solution:}
        Applying finite differences to equation \ref{eqn: prob 3} we can rewrite the problem as

        \begin{equation}
            u_{i+1}\left( \frac{1}{h^2} + \frac{\sin(x_i)}{h}\right) + u_i \left( 1 - \frac{1}{h^2}\right) + u_{i-1}\left( \frac{1}{h^2} - \frac{\sin(x_i)}{h}\right) = \sin(x_i)\cos(x_i),
            \label{eqn: finite_diff_ode}
        \end{equation}

        where $x_i, u_i$ denote the values of x and u at the ith node of the finite difference mesh. $u_0$ and $u_n$ are held at the appropriate boundary values. Solving the system defined by equation \ref{eqn: prob 3} for n = 50 gives the result shown in \ref{fig:sol_3}.
        \begin{figure}[H] 
                \begin{centering}
                    \centering
                        \includegraphics[scale=.75]{Images/problem3_1.png}
                        \caption{Finite difference approximation (n=50) of solution to equation \ref{eqn: prob 3} The exact solution is shown in blue and the numerical solution is shown in red.}
                        \label{fig:sol_3}
                    \end{centering}
                \end{figure}

        Plotting the error, it can once again be seen that the finite difference approximation has second order convergence.
        \begin{figure}[H]
                \begin{centering}
                    \centering
                        \includegraphics[scale=.75]{Images/problem3_2.png}
                        \caption{Convergence plot with $L^2$ norms of absolute error for finite difference approximation in equation \ref{eqn: finite_diff_ode}.}
                    \end{centering}
                \end{figure}
